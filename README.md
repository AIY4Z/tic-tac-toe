## 🎮 How to Play

1. Player 1 uses `X`, Player 2 uses `O`.
2. Click on any square in the grid to make a move.
3. The first player to align 3 symbols (row, column, or diagonal) wins.
4. If the board is full and there's no winner, it's a draw.
5. Click "Restart" to play again.
